
LESSON 1: WHAT IS ELASTICSEARCH ?


The Elasticsearch Platform is a search and analytics platform that helps organizations find, analyze, and act on the data that matters most.

It brings together data from differents systens into one place. So teams can quickly find informations that are relevant to their needs.

    - A security teams can look for signs of suspicious activities
    - The operation teans can focus whether the traffic affects the site performances.


    SEARCH IS THE FOUNDATION OF THE EXPERIENCE.
    It helps teams find relevant informations across large amounts of datas from differents format and places.

    AI BUILDS ON A SEARCH FOUNDATION
    When AI starts with the relevant information, it can help users understand results more quickly by :
    - summerizing those data
    - Highlighting useful details
    - pointing out what matters the most
